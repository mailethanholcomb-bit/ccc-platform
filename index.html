03 How HBOT Works

Four steps, start
to recovery.

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy raises the amount of oxygen dissolved in your blood and delivered to tissue. Under pressure, oxygen reaches areas that circulation alone struggles to supply — which is why a physician may prescribe it for wounds and injuries that have stopped progressing.

  1. 1

    Consult or referral

    You call us directly, or your physician, dentist, surgeon or case manager sends a referral. We gather records and schedule an evaluation.

  2. 2

    Physician treatment plan

    A board-certified hyperbaric physician reviews your history, confirms whether HBOT is appropriate, and sets the pressure, session length and course.

  3. 3

    Chamber sessions

    You breathe 100% oxygen under increased pressure inside the chamber. Sessions typically run 60–120 minutes and are monitored by clinical staff throughout.

  4. 4

    Healing & recovery

    Progress is tracked across the course of treatment and reported back to your referring provider — and, for workers' comp cases, to your case manager.
